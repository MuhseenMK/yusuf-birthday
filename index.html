A Letter to My Brother.

Yusuf, I know you are facing a lot right now. I know you are battling your own tournaments. Just know that in this life, every one of us will face challenges, and the best among us are those who endure them. Whenever you feel down, I want you to remember Allah. Pray. Do zikr. God tests the ones He loves the most.

Continue to pursue your studies. In sha Allah, you will be one of the best lawyers. Avoid distractions. Please follow your mind. Don’t think about what people will say about you. If you want to do something, do it with a clear heart, as long as it doesn’t affect our Deen and our culture. Let me remind you: you are loved. People like you. People love you. Don’t let friends control or choose for you.

And Mumu, if you like, forget about me after you become successful—I will break your leg. I love you to the core. I don’t even know how to express my feelings towards you. Although it’s your birthday, I’m sorry for not giving you a thing. I have nothing to offer like a gift. I’m still crawling in life. But I offer you my prayers.

May the Almighty continue to elevate you. May He prolong the life of Abba and Mom, and bless your family. I am truly sorry I failed as a friend.

I love you, Isuhu.
